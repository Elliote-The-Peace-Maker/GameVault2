HERE TO HELP

Help centre

Quick answers to get you back in the game.

How do I save a game on GameVault?

Open a game page and choose “Add to wishlist.” Your saved games stay in this browser on this device.

How do I follow a creator?

Open Streams, visit the creator profile, and select Follow. The profile will then appear in your followed creators list.

How do I share my profile?

Open your profile and select “Copy profile link.” GameVault copies your local profile URL so you can share it.

Where are my saved clips?

On a creator’s Streams page, choose Favourites. Your saved clips stay in your browser on this device.

How do I change my online status?

Use the status menu in the top bar. Choose Online, Away, Busy, or Invisible.

    Privacy summary

    How this local design preview handles information.

    Privacy settings ↗

    Stored on this device

    This prototype saves preferences, your display name, followed creator state, saved clips, wishlist, forum topics, and drafts in this browser’s local storage. The demo does not send those entries to a GameVault server.

    External links

    Game stores and social platforms open their own websites. Their privacy practices apply when you visit them.

    Clear local data

    You can clear this preview’s saved information by clearing this site’s browser storage. Reset Settings restores the appearance and visibility controls.

    Questions? Visit Help Centre ↗
    GameVault PREVIEW

    Terms summary

    What to expect while exploring this demo.

    Open Help Centre ↗

    Design preview

    GameVault here is a local interface prototype. Profiles, player statistics, creator activity, clips, and leaderboard entries are illustrative sample content and do not represent verified live services.

    Use of external destinations

    Links to game stores and social platforms take you to those services, which have their own terms and accounts. Purchases or account activity there are handled by those providers.

    Local interactions

    Actions such as following, saving clips, posting a forum topic, and writing a support note only update this browser’s local demo data. They do not contact other players or send a support request.
